Two interruptions, and what the extension does with them

Sample article · 4 min read

This page exists so the extension has something to act on that belongs to nobody. It carries the two interruptions people meet most often: a consent bar pinned to the bottom of the window, and a mailing-list modal over the middle of it.

Neither is a picture of a banner. Both are built to the shape the extension's own heuristics look for, so what you see happen to them is the shipping code doing its ordinary job.

With the extension off, both sit there until you deal with them. With it on, the consent bar is answered and the modal is dismissed before the page has finished settling, and the scroll lock the modal installed is lifted.
